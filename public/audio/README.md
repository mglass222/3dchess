# Piece landing sound

`piece-land.wav` is the **second impact** (2.568–2.850 seconds) in
[chess pieces.wav](https://freesound.org/people/simone_ds/sounds/366065/)
by **simone_ds**, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Source: the public high-quality MP3 preview:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/366/366065_6040409-hq.mp3

Modifications: decoded to 48 kHz mono PCM, cropped to the second impact,
gain multiplied by 1.85, 1 ms entrance fade and 30 ms tail fade. The original
pitch and natural impact/decay are retained. This is derived from the MP3 preview,
not the login-only original 24-bit WAV.

Output: 48 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV, 282 ms. The app uses the same recording for
moves and captures, with a modest volume increase for captures.
